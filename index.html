Fénytámasz

Fénytámasz is a private internal automation application used by the owner to publish and manage content on Facebook Pages and Instagram Business accounts that they personally manage.

How the app is used

The app uses Meta APIs to publish posts, images, and videos to Facebook Pages and connected Instagram Business accounts. It is not a public service and does not provide access to external users.

Privacy Policy

This application does not collect, store, or share personal data from Meta users. Access tokens are used solely to perform publishing actions on pages owned by the app administrator.

No personal data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.
